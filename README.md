## 👋 Not technical? Start here — this takes one minute

You don't need a GitHub account, and you don't need to understand any of the technical parts. Here's the whole method:

1. **Click the resource you want** in the list below. Copy the web address from your browser's address bar.
2. **Paste it into whatever AI you use** — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, any of them — along with this:

> *Please read this document and take from it whatever applies to how you and I work together. Set up anything worth setting up, explain anything I need to decide, and tell me what changed.*

3. **That's it.** Your AI does the reading and the technical work. You just get the benefit.

These files are deliberately written to be handed to an AI rather than studied by a person. If your AI can't open a web link, download the file and attach it instead — same result.

## What's here

**[Making your laptop wake up for your AI's scheduled tasks](laptop-wake-for-ai-scheduled-tasks.md)**

If you've told your AI assistant to do something every night at 3am and it quietly never runs, this explains why and how to fix it. Most AI desktop apps can't wake a sleeping laptop — that turns out to be a job you have to hand the operating system yourself.

Includes the setup, the commands, and about a dozen failure modes I only found by hitting them — the ones that look like success. macOS in detail, with pointers for Windows and Linux.

## Found a mistake, or know something I don't?

Please tell me. That's the main reason these live here instead of only on my website.

**To leave a correction, question, or comment:** open an [Issue](../../issues/new). It's just a comment box — you don't need to know anything technical, and you don't need to be a programmer. Say what's wrong or what's missing.

**To suggest an actual edit:** open a Pull Request. GitHub lets you edit any file right in your browser — click the file, click the pencil icon, make your change — and it walks you through the rest. I'll get a notification and can accept it with one click.

Either is genuinely welcome. I'd rather these be right than be mine.
